# Student Bio of LAI Yikai

My name is LAI Yikai, and I was born in the quiet town of Meizhou in Guangdong, China. I graduated with a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from Sun Yat-Sen University, where I spent four years acquiring robust development skills and honing my logical thinking abilities .

I have a deep passion for computer science and programming. To me, programming is like a strategic factory game: it allows me to refine and accumulate effective practices and valuable artifacts, all while delivering the joy of automation . My motto is centered on "Logic and innovation"—I see logic as the pathway to success, and innovation as the essence of a valuable life .

Lately, I've developed a strong interest in finance. I've dedicated significant time to building tools that facilitate data discovery and support investment decision-making. This process has not only allowed me to sharpen my programming skills but has also helped me accumulate substantial experience in software development .

In my free time, I enjoy exploring other interests such as economics, electronics, and photography. Engaging with these hobbies, I always strive to comprehend and articulate their underlying principles through my own analytical lens。

My research interest is Artificial Intelligence and Robotics. I joined a robotics competition (International Competition of Autonomous Running Robots) representing our school and took third place, which was my first time competing in robotics. I was an intern at PCI-Suntek Technology, developing a RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation) system for its OA (Office Automation) system, which provided industrial product production experience.
